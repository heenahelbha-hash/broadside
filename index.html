01 ABOUT THE SHOP

A COMMUNITY PRINT SHOP

Broadside is a community letterpress print shop dedicated to keeping hands-on printing alive.

We're a nonprofit shop above a bicycle repair store, kept alive by dues, workshop fees, and volunteers who show up every week to distribute type, ink and ideas.

No previous printing experience is required. If you've got an idea, we'll help you put it on paper.

02 HOW A JOB GETS MADE

The same three moves, whether it's a wedding invitation or a protest poster.

01
A

COMPOSE

Type is picked letter by letter from the case and locked into a composing stick, backwards and upside down, until the line reads true.

02
■

INK

A thin, even film goes down with a brayer — too much and the letterforms fill in, too little and the impression starves.

03
P

PULL

Paper meets type under real pressure. Every sheet gets pulled by hand, one at a time.
